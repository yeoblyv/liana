Getting started

  1. Open an empty design, or tick Build in a new design in the dialog.
  2. Choose Solid > Create > Liana.
  3. Set the cables, choose the parts and enter your printer bed.
  4. Click Generate. Liana builds the parts as components with User Parameters and a full timeline.

The dialog

GroupWhat it sets
CablesCradles per holder (1 to 15), cable diameter (3 to 25 mm), tooth width, raised teeth that keep each cable in its cradle, and a center T-pin that stiffens combs with 4 or more cradles.
PartsHolder (rail and comb), X-frame and zip-tie mount; which frame sides show a holder (none, straight run, L-turn, T-branch or X-cross); building in a new design.
HolderDepth along the cable, wood screw hole and countersink, wood screw length, M3 hole, M3 nut seat and pin clearance.
X-framePlate thickness and beam width.
Tie mountOuter size and the width of the zip-tie tunnels.
Printer bedPrintable area and the margin kept free at its edges.

The summary at the bottom of the dialog shows the size of every part, the largest cradle count that fits your bed and the screw lengths. Generate stays off while a value is empty or not a number, or while a part does not fit. Reset to defaults restores every value. A box you typed letters into or left empty stays red until you type a number into it, also after a reset.

Changing a design

  • Modify > Change Parameters lists every size with a comment. The sketches are dimensioned with these parameters, so the model follows a change. Parameters marked "(derived)" are calculated from the others.
  • Run Solid > Create > Liana again on a design it built: the dialog opens with that design's settings. Changing only sizes updates the parameters in place. Changing the layout (cradle count, parts, T-pin, raised teeth, frame preview) rebuilds the system in the same design, and edits made to the generated parts by hand are lost.
  • Liana finds its system by markers on its components. Renaming the components is fine; after deleting them the next run builds a new system.

Printing and mounting

  1. Right-click each component (Rail, Comb, X-Frame, Tie Mount) in the browser and choose Save As Mesh.
  2. Print in PLA or PETG with the table side on the bed: the rail, the frame and the tie mount lie on the face that touches the table, the comb on its bottom face. Print one holder first and check the fit.
  3. Push an M3 nut into each nut seat of the rail.
  4. Screw the rail under the table, through the frame if you use one.
  5. Lay the cables into the comb, slide the comb into the rail from the side and tighten the two M3 screws from below.

Liana gives you the geometry; how well it holds depends on your printer, filament and screws. Check that a test holder and the screws suit your table and the weight of your cables, and do not hang anything heavy on it. Liana is provided as is: you print and mount the parts at your own risk.

Hardware

PartHardware (default sizes)
Holder2 countersunk wood screws 3.5 mm (4 when the summary says "4 table screws"), 2 M3 countersunk screws DIN 965 and 2 M3 nuts. The summary shows the M3 length.
X-frame4 countersunk wood screws 3.5 mm at the corners. Holder screws pass through the holder and the frame, so use longer ones there.
Tie mount1 countersunk wood screw 3.5 mm and a zip tie up to 4.8 mm wide.

Files and privacy

Liana collects no data and makes no network connections. It keeps the last dialog settings (last_settings.json) and the log of the last build (build_log.txt) in its own add-in folder, and the settings of a system inside the design. Uninstalling Liana removes the two files. The full privacy policy is in the Privacy policy group of the dialog and at github.com/yeoblyv/liana/blob/main/PRIVACY.md.
